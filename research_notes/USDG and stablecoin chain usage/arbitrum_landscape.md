## KQ1. Total stablecoin market cap on Arbitrum today, per-asset breakdown, trend, and ranking vs other chains

### Takeaway
Arbitrum One holds roughly $3.8–4.1B of stablecoins on 10 Oct 2026 (DefiLlama chain total $3.77B; DefiLlama per-asset sum and Stablecoin Beat both ~$4.1B), ranking 7th among chains behind Ethereum, Tron, Solana, BSC, Hyperliquid L1 and Base, and just ahead of Polygon. ### Inferences
- The ~2x gap between DefiLlama (~$4B) and Artemis/Foundation (~$7.7–10B) is best explained by Hyperliquid-bridge attribution: DefiLlama reassigns USDC locked in the Arbitrum-side Hyperliquid bridge to "Hyperliquid L1" (its bridge page shows $5.05B attributed to Hyperliquid L1 vs $853M Arbitrum), while Artemis/Token Terminal appear to count that USDC as Arbitrum supply. DefiLlama's sharp USDC drop from $1.14B (Jan 2025) to $215M (Apr 2025) and subsequent rebound is consistent with a methodology change rather than real flows. Treat "Arbitrum stablecoin supply ex-Hyperliquid-bridge" ≈ $4B as the organic number.
- Excluding the Hyperliquid pass-through, Arbitrum's organic stablecoin float has been roughly flat at $3.6–4.7B since Aug 2025, with USDC ~55–60%, USDT0 ~20%, and two newer issuers (PYUSD, USDai) together now ~17%.
- The USD818–865M of USDT is entirely USDT0 (LayerZero OFT) since the Jan 2025 upgrade; DefiLlama and L2BEAT classify it as externally bridged.

### Gaps
- No single authoritative "native USDC vs USDC.e" split from Circle; the $49M USDC.e figure is DefiLlama's bridgedTo field, not an Arbiscan read.
- Could not obtain Artemis or Token Terminal raw series directly (paywalled/JS); their numbers are cited via secondary coverage.
- DefiLlama's two endpoints disagree ($3.77B vs $4.13B) on the same day; cause not documented.

## KQ3. Biggest stablecoin sinks on Arbitrum and natural first integrations; listing processes at Aave, Morpho, Fluid, GMX, Pendle

### Takeaway
Stablecoin demand on Arbitrum is concentrated in lending (Aave V3 ~$508M TVL, Fluid ~$174M lending+DEX, Compound ~$70M, Morpho, Dolomite), Spark Savings ($307M), GMX V2 ($202M), Pendle ($171M), Uniswap V3/V4 (~$175M) and the Hyperliquid bridge ($388M Arbitrum-side); the USDG launch playbook (Fluid + Uniswap pools, GMX GLV vault, Morpho curated vaults with Maple syrupUSDG, Kraken on/off-ramp, Stargate/LI.FI routing, DRIP incentives) is the de facto template. ## KQ6. Technical path and costs for an issuer to deploy a native stablecoin on Arbitrum (contracts, bridge, oracles, verification, DEX/lending, CEX)

### Takeaway
On Arbitrum One an issuer has three routes: (a) do nothing and let the canonical standard gateway auto-deploy a bridged ERC-20 (what produced USDC.e); (b) deploy its own L2 token implementing `IArbToken` and register it with the generic-custom gateway via `ICustomToken.registerTokenToL2` + router mapping (DAO-governed on Arbitrum One); or (c) issue natively on L2 with its own mint/burn (USDC via CCTP, PYUSD, USDG) and move supply via issuer or OFT bridges (USDT0 via LayerZero). Circle's Bridged USDC Standard is only for new Orbit chains, not Arbitrum One. Commercial costs (oracle feeds, DEX seeding, incentives) dominate; contract/registration gas is trivial.

### Cited Findings
- Arbitrum token-bridge options: standard gateway ("automatic deployment of a standard ERC-20 on Arbitrum, no configuration required"), generic-custom gateway ("custom functionality in your child chain token while using Arbitrum's built-in gateway"), custom gateway ("specialized gateway logic"). — [Arbitrum docs: bridge tokens overview](https://docs.arbitrum.io/devs-how-tos/bridge-tokens/how-to-bridge-tokens-overview)
- Mechanics: any parent-chain ERC-20 not registered to a custom gateway can be bridged permissionlessly through `StandardERC20Gateway` (user calls `L1GatewayRouter.outboundTransferCustomRefund`; L1 escrow + retryable ticket; `L2ERC20Gateway.finalizeInboundTransfer` mints a `StandardArbERC20`). Generic-custom: parent token implements `ICustomToken` (incl. `isArbitrumEnabled`), child token implements `IArbToken` (`bridgeMint`/`bridgeBurn` callable only by `L2CustomGateway`, `l1Address`); registration via `L1CustomGateway.registerTokenToL2` then `L1GatewayRouter.setGateway`, which "can be done as chain-owner registrations through an Arbitrum DAO proposal". A token that "can increase its supply (i.e., mint) directly on the child chain" and must be withdrawable "will probably require its own special gateway". Withdrawals: 7-day dispute window then `Outbox.executeTransaction`. — [Arbitrum docs: token bridging deep dive](https://docs.arbitrum.io/how-arbitrum-works/deep-dives/token-bridging)
- Native USDC precedent: canonical bridge mints USDC.e; native USDC is minted/burned through Circle CCTP; USDC.e liquidity expected to keep migrating. — [Arbitrum docs: USDC on Arbitrum One](https://docs.arbitrum.io/arbitrum-bridge/usdc-arbitrum-one)
- USDT0 precedent: Tether upgraded the L2 USDT contract to a LayerZero OFT (Jan 2025), detaching it from the canonical gateway; >$1B sits in the LayerZero lockbox; the DAO then had to pass a Constitutional AIP to disable the legacy gateway, with a one-week L2 waiting period after Tally. Trust assumptions shift to LayerZero DVNs and lockbox owner liveness. — [Arbitrum forum: Disable Legacy Tether Bridge AIP](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/constitutional-aip-disable-legacy-tether-bridge/29503)
- USDG/PYUSD precedent: USDG "natively issued on Arbitrum One", mint/redeem via Paxos accounts, cross-chain via Stargate (LayerZero) and LI.FI; PYUSD deployed natively on Arbitrum Jul 2025. — [Arbitrum blog](https://blog.arbitrum.io/usdg-is-live-on-arbitrum/); [PayPal newsroom](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2025-07-17-PayPal-USD-Expands-to-Arbitrum-Bringing-Layer-2-Efficiency-to-Multi-Chain-Development-Stack)
- Bridged USDC Standard (Orbit chains only): contracts `L1USDCGateway`/`L1OrbitUSDCGateway` + `L2USDCGateway` + Circle reference bridged token; deploy with token-bridge-contracts v1.2.0 via `yarn deploy:usdc-token-bridge`; later Circle upgrade requires pausing deposits/withdrawals, revoking the gateway minter role, `setBurner`/`setBurnAmount`, `transferUSDCRoles`, `burnLockedUSDC`. Explicitly "will not be used on existing chains governed by the DAO", i.e. not Arbitrum One. — [Arbitrum docs: Bridged USDC Standard](https://docs.arbitrum.io/launch-arbitrum-chain/integrations/bridged-usdc)
- Oracles: Chainlink supported on Arbitrum; custom/long-tail Chainlink feeds are sales-led with unquoted setup and node compensation; GMX requires Chainlink Data Streams feeds before listing a market; RedStone feeds quoted at ~$12.8k–$32.8k/yr (USDC-invoiced) in an Inverse Finance proposal; Pyth is pull-based. — [Arbitrum docs: Chainlink](https://docs.arbitrum.io/for-devs/oracles/chainlink/); [GMX market launch (older)](https://gmxio.substack.com/p/gmx-launches-an-eth-usd-wsteth-usde); [Inverse Finance proposal](https://www.inverse.finance/governance/proposals/mills/316)
- Lending listing: Aave requires ARFC → Snapshot → AIP with Chaos Labs/LlamaRisk sign-off (USDai was blocked for peg/redemption reasons); Morpho vaults and Pendle markets are curator/permissionless; GMX uses single-token GLV vaults (USDG) with Chaos Labs parameters; Fluid via Instadapp governance. — [Aave ARFC USDai](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260); [Arbitrum blog USDG](https://blog.arbitrum.io/usdg-is-live-on-arbitrum/)
- CEX support: Kraken supports USDG deposits/withdrawals on Arbitrum One at launch; Gemini added USDT0-on-Arbitrum 21 May 2026; Binance/Bybit/Bitget/OKX hot wallets hold $760M/$137M/$60M/$27M on Arbitrum (all assets). — [Arbitrum blog USDG](https://blog.arbitrum.io/usdg-is-live-on-arbitrum/); [Gemini](https://www.gemini.com/blog/gemini-has-expanded-its-tether-offering-usat-tokenized-gold-and-usdt0-on-arbitrum); [DefiLlama protocols API](https://api.llama.fi/protocols)
- Incentive economics for a chain-aligned launch: DRIP Season 1 spent 16.7M ARB over ~5.5 months on lending-market incentives; USDG launch carries >$10M of DRIP incentives and a 100M ARB request. — [DRIP Jan 2026 update](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/drip-january-2026-update/30546); [Arbitrum blog USDG](https://blog.arbitrum.io/usdg-is-live-on-arbitrum/)

### Inferences
