# USDC and USDT: chain distribution and protocol-level usage (state as of 10 Oct 2026, Arbitrum focus)

Method note: unless stated otherwise, per-chain supply figures below were pulled directly from the DefiLlama stablecoins API on 2026-10-10 (`https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoins`, `/stablecoin/1` for USDT, `/stablecoin/2` for USDC, `/stablecoincharts/{chain}?stablecoin={id}`). DefiLlama's "circulating" per chain = natively minted on that chain minus what has been bridged out of it, plus what was bridged in. This matters for Arbitrum and Hyperliquid (see Q3). Protocol holdings come from `https://api.llama.fi/protocol/{slug}` (`tokensInUsd` for the chain plus the `-borrowed` series; "gross" = supplied incl. borrowed) and from Aavescan. Arbiscan token pages were fetched 2026-10-10 but the holder tables are behind JS/403, so address-level holder rankings are a gap.

## Q1. Total supply of USDC and USDT and breakdown by chain; which chains grew/shrank over the past year

### Takeaway
On 10 Oct 2026 USDT is ~$184.2B and USDC ~$73.1B (DefiLlama), i.e. roughly 2.5:1; USDT sits ~95% on Tron + Ethereum + BSC while USDC is ~62% Ethereum with Hyperliquid (now #2 at ~$7.1B natively minted), Solana, Base and Arbitrum behind. Over the past 12 months the big movers were Tron (+$17B USDT), Hyperliquid (USDC went from 100% bridged-from-Arbitrum to ~95% natively minted), Solana USDC (−$4.5B), Ethereum USDT (−$6B), and the near-complete deflation of USDT on Plasma (−$3.6B) and Stable, while Circle's own Arc chain appeared at ~$0.5B USDC within three weeks of launch.

## Q2. Which protocols hold the most USDC and USDT (by chain)

### Takeaway
By far the largest single on-chain "holder" of USDC is Hyperliquid (≈$7.1B natively minted on Hyperliquid L1 after the mid-2026 migration from the Arbitrum bridge), followed by Sky's PSM/lending (~$4.4B USDC on Ethereum), Morpho (>$2.8B USDC across chains, mostly Base and Ethereum) and Aave V3 Ethereum (~$2.4B USDC, ~$2.7B USDT). Aave V3 remains the largest DeFi venue for USDT (Ethereum ~$2.7B; Plasma USDT0 ~$0.33–0.73B). A hack-driven outflow in April 2026 roughly halved Aave V3 Ethereum's stablecoin deposits, letting Morpho overtake Aave for USDC.

## Q3. Arbitrum: native USDC vs USDC.e vs USDT/USDT0 today, trend since Hyperliquid became the largest holder, deepest stablecoin liquidity

### Takeaway
On 10 Oct 2026 Arbitrum carries ~$2.67B native USDC, only ~$48M USDC.e, ~$824M USDT0 (all Arbitrum USDT was upgraded to the USDT0 OFT in Jan 2025), plus ~$359M PYUSD and ~$100M USDS. The Hyperliquid bridge, which held a peak $5.64B USDC (Sep 2025, ~70% of Arbitrum's USDC), was drained to ~$0.3–0.4B in June 2026 as Hyperliquid moved to natively minted USDC; net of that, Arbitrum's "own" USDC has been stable at ~$2.1–2.4B for a year. The deepest stablecoin venues on Arbitrum are now Aave V3 (~$180M USDC), Spark Savings vaults (~$307M), Pendle, Fluid, GMX (~$85M USDC) and Uniswap V3 — each an order of magnitude below the old bridge balance.
