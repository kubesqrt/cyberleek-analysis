# Chain and protocol footprint of major stablecoins other than USDC/USDT/USDG (as of 2026-10-10)

Data convention: unless otherwise dated, supply figures below are from the DefiLlama stablecoins API (`https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoins` and `/stablecoin/{id}`) pulled on 2026-10-10. DefiLlama "circulating" per chain counts natively minted supply on that chain; bridged balances (e.g. canonical-bridge USDe or GHO on Arbitrum) are tracked separately and may be omitted from the per-chain circulating number. "YTD" = change from 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-10, computed from the DefiLlama history endpoint. Where other sources conflict, both are given.

Paxos-issued family (relevant to USDG consolidation): **PYUSD** (PayPal USD), **USDG** (Global Dollar), **USDL** (Lift Dollar), **USDP** (Pax Dollar). All four issued by Paxos entities (Paxos Trust Company NY for PYUSD/USDP; Paxos Digital Singapore under MAS and Paxos Issuance Europe under MiCA for USDG; Paxos International (ADGM) for USDL). — [The Block on USDG EU](https://www.theblock.co/post/360497/global-dollar-network-expands-with-eu-launch-of-usdg-stablecoin-by-paxos-with-backing-from-robinhood-kraken-and-other); [The Block on PYUSD/Paxos](https://www.theblock.co/post/371321/paypal-pyusd-stablecoin-expands-blockchains-layerzero)

## Key Question 1: Per-stablecoin profile — issuer, supply, chains, protocols, distribution, Arbitrum status

### Takeaway
As of 2026-10-10 the non-USDC/USDT/USDG field is led by USDS ($6.8–7.0B), USDe ($4.8B), DAI ($4.75B), USD1 ($4.3B), PYUSD ($2.9B) and RLUSD ($2.4B); most of them are overwhelmingly Ethereum-mainnet assets, and apart from PYUSD ($359M) and USDai ($344M) essentially none has meaningful native supply on Arbitrum. Several names on the request list have collapsed or been sunset since late 2025 (deUSD, USDX, USR, USDH).
