Revenue vs Valuation

Protocol revenue (DeFiLlama) joined with token market data (CoinGecko) — does the token capture the cash flow?
Loading DeFiLlama + CoinGecko data…

Method. Revenue = DeFiLlama dailyRevenue (protocol take, not total user fees), children summed into parent protocols. WoW = last 7d vs prior 7d; MoM = last 30d vs prior 30d; vs 1y = last 30d vs trailing-1y monthly average. Annualized revenue = 30d × 12.17. P/S = market cap ÷ annualized revenue; P/F = FDV ÷ annualized revenue (lower = cheaper per revenue dollar; only meaningful when the token has a claim on the business). Capture = share of protocol revenue paid to token holders (DeFiLlama holders revenue: burns, buybacks, staker/locker distributions) — the closest on-chain proxy for “does the token capture the revenue”. Mcap/FDV shows dilution overhang. Chains (L1 gas revenue) are excluded; CEX rows are hidden by default via the token filter unless their token has holder revenue. Prices/mcap/FDV: CoinGecko.
